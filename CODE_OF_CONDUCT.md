# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Welcome people with different
backgrounds and experience levels. Critique ideas and evidence rather than
people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized conduct, and
publication of another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer using the contact path in
`SECURITY.md` when confidentiality matters. The maintainer may edit or remove
contributions, limit participation, or ban contributors when necessary to
protect the community.

This policy applies in project repositories, discussions, issue trackers, and
other spaces where someone represents the project.
